Pocket guide
How to travel
W A S D
Walk. In the cab, W/S raises or lowers power.
E
Board nearby train. At the destination, deliver while stopped. Otherwise leave while stopped.
Space
Hold to brake. Begin braking before the platform.
C / R
Switch cab/chase view. Reverse direction while stopped.
Mouse / arrows
Click world to capture mouse. Drag to look if capture is unavailable. Arrow keys also look.
Esc / H
Pause / guide. Escape releases mouse.
F
Centre the train camera on the railway.
B / V
Add coal to the fire / sound the whistle. Ease power for bridges and crossings. Pressure supplies the engine’s pull.
T
Use parcels and noticeboards on foot. Hold T for lamps or the water crane. Enter waiting rooms, sit at benches, or cast and reel at the fishing jetty. J: Around the station gives directions.
X
Put away the fishing rod, stop a refill or stand from a bench. E leaves the waiting room.
1 2 3
At a post-sorting board, choose the sack matching the letter’s address: Pinebank, Bellweather, Stonehaven.
L
Light or put away your hand lantern. Night visibility can be adjusted in Settings.
J
Open your journal, station noticeboard and paint workshop. Discover places on foot along station platforms.
Destination and interaction hints appear at the bottom. Settings provides weather, portable saves, and recovery.
